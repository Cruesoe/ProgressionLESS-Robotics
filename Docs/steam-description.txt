[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3736402055]Progression: Robotics[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Overhauls robot research, so each mech, drone and piece of mechanitor gear unlocks in its proper tech era.
[*]Mechs unlock behind the mechtech tier that makes their subcore: basic, standard or high.
[*]Mechanitor gear, mech boosters and mech facilities get their own steps through the tiers.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Robotics adds up to [b]125 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork groups them into [b]at most 43[/b], and the mechtech tiers keep their vanilla names:
[list]
[*][b]Hunter pack[/b], [b]Hunter drone traps and packs[/b], [b]Extinguisher drone[/b], [b]Work drones[/b] and [b]Combat drones[/b] (Industrial).
[*][b]Heavy labor[/b], [b]Service[/b], [b]Front-line[/b] and [b]Ranged mechs[/b]; [b]Advanced labor[/b], [b]Heavy combat[/b] and [b]Invader mechs[/b]; [b]War machines[/b]; [b]Mechanitor gear[/b] in three steps; [b]Mech booster and field devices[/b]; [b]Apex mechs[/b], [b]Conquerors[/b] and [b]Commander mechs[/b] (Spacer).
[*][b]Ultra combat mechs[/b], [b]Ultra mechanitor gear[/b], [b]Remote mechlink upgrades[/b] and [b]Apocriton-class mechs[/b] (Ultra).
[/list]
Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support:
[list]
[*]Biotech and Odyssey.
[*]Alpha Mechs (with Biotech).
[*]Apex Mechanoids.
[*]Machines of War.
[*]Mechanoid Invaders.
[*]More Hunter Drones (with Odyssey).
[*]Vanilla Quests Expanded - Drone Factory.
[*]Extinguisher Drone.
[*]Pal5k's Hunter, Scrambler and Logistics Mechanoids.
[*]Excavator Mechanoid, Mechanoid Waiter and Surrogate Mechanoid.
[*]Simple Additions: Starfire Boss.
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]

[b]Switching mid-game isn't recommended.[/b] Many of the original's research projects are merged or removed, so swapping Progression: Robotics for this fork in an existing save can lose research progress. Start a new game when you switch.
