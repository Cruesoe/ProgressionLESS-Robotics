# ProgressionLESS: Robotics

**All the Progression goodness, less research.**

Research overhaul for robots, so each mech, drone and piece of mechanitor gear unlocks in its proper tech era.

The "LESS" is less research: Progression: Robotics adds up to 125 research projects depending on your mods. This fork groups them into at most 48 themed projects, such as Front-line mechs, Ranged mechs, Mechanitor gear, Hunter drone traps and Conquerors. Everything stays in the same era and at the same mech tier, and still needs the other research it did before.

Forked from [Progression: Robotics](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3736402055) by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [The Progression Modpack](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585).
